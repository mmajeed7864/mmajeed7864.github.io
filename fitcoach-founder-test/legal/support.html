FitCoach support

Help without the runaround.

Get help with the 18+ engineering preview, local data, workout logging, voice playback, food lookup, privacy, and accessibility.

Last updated August 31, 2026
Engineering preview status

Local workout and nutrition tools can work without an account. Cloud accounts, sync, subscriptions, and native health connections are unavailable unless the app confirms production configuration is live.

Contact support

Email the issue, device, operating system, browser or app version, and the steps that caused it. Do not include passwords, sign-in codes, API keys, medical records, or payment-card details. No guaranteed response time is offered for the engineering preview.

support@symbioai.dev

Account or sync problem

The current preview fails closed when production account configuration is unavailable. A sign-in control does not prove an account exists. Continue locally, avoid repeated code requests, and contact support if the app specifically confirms an account but cannot sync. See Delete FitCoach data for current controls.

Coach does not speak automatically

  1. Confirm Read replies aloud is enabled in trainer settings.
  2. Tap once inside Voice Room so the browser can unlock audio playback.
  3. Check silent mode, media volume, Bluetooth output, and browser microphone permission.
  4. Keep the page visible during the first response; mobile browsers can pause audio in the background.
  5. Use the visible text or replay control if premium voice falls back to device speech.

Browser speech recognition may use the browser, operating system, or its speech service. FitCoach does not persist microphone audio in the local profile.

Barcode or food search is unavailable

Provider lookup can fail because of coverage, rate limits, network conditions, or an unavailable upstream service. Enter the package label manually and review the serving and portion. Open Food Facts records are community-contributed; USDA FoodData Central records are reference data. Neither replaces the package label when exact values matter.

Workout or motion guide problem

  • Refresh FitCoach and reopen the exercise.
  • Check the network if a motion video has not been viewed before; videos stream directly rather than being stored offline.
  • Use the technique poster and written setup while video is unavailable.
  • Do not continue a movement that causes sharp pain, dizziness, fainting, chest pain, or another urgent symptom.

Local data and recovery

Use Export my data before browser cleanup or device migration. Clearing browser site data, private-browsing sessions, device cleanup, or uninstalling a browser can remove local FitCoach history. Cloud recovery is unavailable until backend account and sync configuration is live.

Safety and emergencies

FitCoach support does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, crisis counseling, or emergency response. For urgent symptoms or immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For medical, injury, nutrition, or mental-health concerns, contact an appropriately qualified professional.

Policies and data requests

Read the Privacy Notice and Terms of Use. Use the data deletion page for local reset instructions and cloud-account status.
