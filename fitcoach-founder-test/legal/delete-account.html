Data controls

Remove FitCoach data.

The current preview stores its profile locally. Cloud account deletion and device sync are unavailable until production backend configuration is live.

Last updated August 31, 2026
No active cloud account service in this preview

A visible sign-in control does not mean a cloud account exists. Authentication fails closed while public backend configuration is unavailable.

1. Export first if needed

Open FitCoach, go to Profile, and choose Export my data before resetting if you want a copy of the local profile. Keep exports private because they can contain workout, nutrition, and Coach history.

2. Delete local FitCoach data

  1. Open FitCoach on the browser and device whose data you want to remove.
  2. Go to Profile and choose Reset FitCoach.
  3. Read the warning and confirm the reset.
  4. If the app is inaccessible, use the browser’s site-data settings to remove storage for this FitCoach site.

The FitCoach reset removes FitCoach profiles, workout and nutrition history, Coach history, preferences, recovery copies, theme state, and session-only media previews from that browser. It does not clear unrelated website data.

Local browser data does not automatically synchronize across browsers or devices. Repeat the reset on every browser or device where you used the preview.

3. Cloud account requests

Cloud account creation, sync, and server deletion are unavailable until production backend configuration is live. If FitCoach later confirms that you have a cloud account, the production app must provide an in-app deletion path and this page must provide an outside-the-app request path.

You may email support@symbioai.dev from the address associated with the account. Support will first determine whether a configured cloud account exists. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, government identifiers, medical records, or payment-card details.

4. What a future cloud deletion must cover

When cloud accounts launch, deletion must remove the account and associated synced FitCoach profile, workouts, nutrition entries, settings, Coach history retained for that account, and private media records, subject only to retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or transaction records. The production policy must state exact retention periods before account launch.

5. Store subscriptions are separate

Deleting local or cloud FitCoach data does not cancel billing managed by Apple or Google. If subscriptions launch, manage or cancel them in your App Store or Google Play account. FitCoach must explain active billing before account deletion and offer immediate deletion even if a subscription remains active.

6. Other platform records

Apple, Google, browser vendors, nutrition providers, or other services may retain records under their own policies. FitCoach cannot delete records controlled solely by another service. You can manage browser permissions, HealthKit or Health Connect access, and store purchase history through the relevant platform controls.
