What is not included in the fixed price

Fixed price means Symbio's build labor is fixed. It does not mean outside vendors are free. To avoid monthly Symbio invoices, Island Vape Shop should own and pay these accounts directly:

  • Domain: usually about $15-$25/year.
  • Hosting: usually $0-$30/month depending on setup and traffic.
  • Age/ID verification: AgeChecker.net currently lists $25/month plus $0.50 per accepted verification; other vendors may price differently.
  • SMS texting: usage-based if order-ready texts are enabled.
  • High-risk merchant processing: only needed if online payment is activated later.
  • Legal/compliance review: optional but recommended for smoke-shop/tobacco/hemp rules.

Symbio can help connect these tools, but the accounts should stay in the client's name so the client is not locked into a monthly Symbio plan.

Compliance-first setup

The safest launch path is pickup-only, not shipping. The website is built around three checks:

1. Browse gate21+ date-of-birth check before entering the site.
2. Checkout verificationThird-party ID/age verification before reservation is accepted.
3. Store pickup checkStaff verifies valid 21+ ID in person before handing over product.

Symbio builds the workflow. Island Vape Shop remains responsible for final legal compliance, product rules, and staff procedures.
